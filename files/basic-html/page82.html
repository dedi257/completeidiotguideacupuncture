          Blood has an additional meaning in Oriental Medicine than what most of us grew up
          with. Blood is seen as a form of Qi that flows through our veins and carries the energy
          of Qi within to nourish and moisten your body. A common strategy in Oriental Medicine

          for dry skin is to eat foods and herbs that strengthen the blood aspect in your body. A
          relative deficiency in blood can also lead to mental restlessness, forgetfulness, or
          insomnia. These correspondences have been observed, treated, and explained by the
          concept of the energy that your blood possesses.


               Deficient Qi. Symptoms of a deficient Qi include fatigue, depression, pale

               Stuck Qi. If your Qi is “stuck,” you’re likely to feel a dull pain, bloating, or

               Deficient blood. A condition involving deficient blood may cause dizziness, pale

               Stuck blood. If your blood is “stuck,” you’ll probably feel a localized, fixed,
